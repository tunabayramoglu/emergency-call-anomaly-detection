## 1. What The system is
Multimodal emergency-call **anomaly detector**: the anomaly = a mismatch between **how something is said** (voice emotion) and **what is said** (text content) → signals RISK. Two audio models feed a fusion:
- **ASR** (mHuBERT-147 + LoRA + weighted-sum + CTC + KenLM) → transcript → **text_risk** (BERT zero-shot)
- **SER** (mHuBERT-147 + LoRA + weighted-sum head) → emotion → **voice_risk**
- **Fusion**: combine the two risks → anomaly. Current baseline = rule-based **late fusion (XOR / mismatch)**.

Both audio models share the **same frozen mHuBERT-147 backbone** (two LoRA adapters + heads).

## 3. Fusion dataset — READY
Location: **`_Staj/fusion/dataset_final.jsonl` — 9,740 labeled utterances.**

**Framing (locked):** NOT end-to-end (no audio). It is a **fusion-combiner benchmark at the symbolic (emotion-label, text) level**, because the pipeline reduces audio to (text, emotion). Present it honestly as such, not as end-to-end.

**How it was built:**
1. `seeds.py` — compositional, **hard-constrained** seed generator: WHO (20 caller profiles) × WHAT (event → sets content_risk + category) × HOW (emotion from SER's 6) × channel/context/twist/dispatcher/register. Constraints guarantee coherent combos (no LLM "skip" escape). `seeds.jsonl` = 1500 seeds.
2. Generation: `generate.py` + `prompts.py` (MUST-framed, seed injected by driver so the LLM never picks its own seed). Ran with **~10 models** (deepseek-v3.2/v4, qwen3.x family, glm-5) → `utterances_*.jsonl` → `utterances_merged.jsonl` (9,758). `run_multi_model.py`.
3. Judging: **multi-judge ensemble** (many models, majority vote) via `run_multi_judge.py` → `dataset_judge_*.jsonl` (23 files) → `dataset_final.jsonl`. Each row carries `judge_count`, `anomaly_votes`, `judge_agreement`, `judge_models`.
4. Quality review: `check_quality.py`, `review_batch_*`, `judge_review_*` audit artifacts.

**Schema (per utterance / row):**
`seed_id, profile, event, target_emotion, content_risk_seed, text, gen_emotion, gen_content_risk, uid, source_model, judge_content_risk, judge_voice_risk, anomaly, reason, judge_model, judge_count, judge_agreement, judge_models, anomaly_votes`

**Distribution (final):**
- `anomaly`: normal 4926 (50.6%), anomaly 3569 (36.6%), borderline 1245 (12.8%)
- `gen_emotion`: neutral 2267, confusion 1978, fear 1616, panic 1399, urgency 1386, distress 1094 (all 6 covered)
- `judge_content_risk`: high 5650 / low 2482 / mid 1608 · `judge_voice_risk`: high 5586 / low 4151

**Key design decisions (do NOT re-litigate):**
- emotion axis = SER's 6 classes (so the app's SER output space matches).
- content_risk comes from the EVENT; emotion sampled freely within profile's allowed set → the mismatch has a built-in REASON (the profile).
- anomaly label = **holistic judge** (NOT plain XOR), else degenerate (all fusion methods tie). Judge is a **separate lens** (no access to seed/intended label) to avoid generator↔judge bias. Ensemble of judges + agreement.
- The `borderline` class exists (mid content or mild incongruence).
